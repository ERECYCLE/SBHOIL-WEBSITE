Sectors we serve

SBHLOIL is focused on commercial sectors where vehicles, machinery and equipment depend on reliable lubricant supply. Requirements are reviewed according to the application, equipment, operating conditions, quantity and location.

01 / Automotive

Automotive

Automotive customers can include vehicle owners, garages, service operations and businesses maintaining passenger and light commercial vehicles. SBHLOIL can discuss engine-oil and lubricant requirements based on the vehicle and required specification.

Typical applications

  • Passenger vehicles
  • Light commercial vehicles
  • Garages and service centers
  • Automotive maintenance

Useful information

  • Vehicle make and model
  • Engine type
  • Required grade, if known
  • Quantity and location
