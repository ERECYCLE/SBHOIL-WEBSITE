SBHL / DISTRIBUTION MODEL

From supply to repeat delivery.

SBHL's operating concept connects product sourcing with shipment preparation, movement into Burkina Faso, customer distribution and the next supply cycle.

The five-step cycle

01 — Supply

Source and consolidate products according to customer requirements and available supply.

02 — Load

Prepare the shipment, commercial documentation and logistics information required for the movement.

03 — Ship

Coordinate transportation into Burkina Faso and manage the planned shipment cycle.

04 — Distribute

Deliver to commercial customers, resellers, fleet operators and other approved buyers.

05 — Repeat

Reconcile the cycle, review demand and prepare the next order or recurring supply requirement.
