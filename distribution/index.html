SBHLOIL / DISTRIBUTION

Distribution Model

SBHLOIL uses a controlled cycle: source and consolidate → prepare shipment and documentation → transport and receive → distribute → collect → reconcile shipment economics → recycle working capital.

01

SUPPLY

Confirm product, quantity, specification, supplier terms and landed-cost assumptions.

02

LOAD

Prepare packing, commercial and transport documentation.

03

SHIP

Move the shipment into Burkina Faso through the selected route.

04

RECEIVE

Inspect, document and reconcile received inventory.

05

DISTRIBUTE

Deliver to resellers, garages, fleets and industrial customers.
