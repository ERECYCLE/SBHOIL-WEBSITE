The five-step cycle

SBHLOIL's distribution concept is designed around a repeatable supply process. Each order is reviewed according to product availability, quantity, logistics requirements, customer location and delivery timing.

01 / Supply

Supply

The cycle begins by identifying customer requirements and sourcing or consolidating the requested lubricant products. Product specifications and availability are reviewed before commercial commitment.

Key activities

  • Review product requirements
  • Confirm quantity and packaging
  • Check supplier availability
  • Prepare commercial supply details

Customer inputs

  • Product or application
  • Required volume
  • Delivery destination
  • Timing
↑ Back to distribution steps
02 / Load

Load

Once supply is confirmed, the shipment is prepared for movement. This stage covers loading coordination and the commercial or logistics documentation needed for the planned shipment.

Preparation

  • Order consolidation
  • Packaging and loading planning
  • Shipment documentation
  • Logistics coordination

Objective

  • Accurate shipment quantities
  • Clear documentation
  • Planned movement
  • Customer delivery readiness
↑ Back to distribution steps
03 / Ship

Ship

The shipment is coordinated for transportation into Burkina Faso. Logistics timing and delivery planning depend on the route, shipment size, supplier arrangements and applicable transport conditions.

Shipment planning

  • Transport coordination
  • Route and timing planning
  • Shipment status follow-up
  • Delivery coordination

Information tracked

  • Shipment quantities
  • Destination
  • Expected timing
  • Customer delivery requirements
↑ Back to distribution steps
04 / Distribute

Distribute

Products are delivered to commercial customers, resellers, fleet operators and other approved buyers according to the agreed supply arrangement.

Customer channels

  • Commercial customers
  • Resellers
  • Fleet operators
  • Industrial and project buyers

Delivery focus

  • Correct product and quantity
  • Destination coordination
  • Delivery timing
  • Order follow-up
↑ Back to distribution steps
05 / Repeat

Repeat

After delivery, the cycle can be reconciled and used to prepare the next order. For recurring customers, historical demand and expected requirements can inform future supply planning.

Cycle review

  • Reconcile delivered quantities
  • Review customer requirements
  • Identify recurring demand
  • Prepare the next cycle

Recurring supply

  • Fleet replenishment
  • Industrial consumption
  • Reseller restocking
  • Project-based requirements
