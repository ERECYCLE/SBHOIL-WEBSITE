01 / Engine Oils

Engine Oils

Engine oils are designed to lubricate and protect engines during operation by helping manage friction, heat and contaminants. SBHLOIL can discuss engine-oil requirements for passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles and fleet applications and then identify the appropriate available product for the equipment.

Typical applications

  • Passenger cars and light vehicles
  • Commercial vehicles and service vehicles
  • Company and transport fleets
  • Routine maintenance and oil-change programs

Information to provide

  • Vehicle or equipment make and model
  • Engine type and required specification, if known
  • Number of vehicles or expected volume
  • Delivery location and timing
