How SBHLOIL supports customers

Our service approach is designed for customers who need dependable lubricant sourcing and distribution support rather than a one-size-fits-all transaction. Requirements can be discussed by application, volume, location and timing.

01 / Product Supply

Product Supply

Product Supply covers the process of understanding the customer's lubricant requirement, identifying the appropriate available category or product, organizing quantities and preparing the commercial supply discussion.

Useful for

  • One-time product requirements
  • Recurring lubricant orders
  • Commercial and industrial buyers
  • Resellers and service businesses

Information to provide

  • Product or application
  • Quantity or expected volume
  • Delivery location
  • Required timing

02 / Distribution

Distribution

Distribution covers the planned movement of lubricant products to customers and commercial channels in Burkina Faso. The process can include shipment coordination, delivery planning and customer distribution support.

Distribution support

  • Customer delivery planning
  • Reseller and commercial supply
  • Shipment coordination
  • Delivery-cycle follow-up

Planning inputs

  • Destination and delivery location
  • Order quantity
  • Preferred delivery window
  • Product and packaging requirements

03 / Fleet Supply

Fleet Supply

Fleet Supply is designed for transport operators and organizations with recurring lubricant consumption. SBHLOIL can discuss fleet size, vehicle types, consumption patterns and preferred replenishment cycles.

Typical customers

  • Transport companies
  • Bus and passenger fleets
  • Commercial vehicle operators
  • Large vehicle-maintenance operations

Planning information

  • Number and type of vehicles
  • Current oil requirements, if known
  • Estimated monthly volume
  • Operating locations

  • Distribution partners
