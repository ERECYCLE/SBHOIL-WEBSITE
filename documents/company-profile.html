SBHLOIL / PROFILE

Company Profile

Société Bilal Hamiadou Linkoné (SBHL), presented publicly as SBHLOIL, is developing an oil and lubricants import, trading and distribution operation based in Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso.

Operating focus

Passenger vehicles, heavy-duty transport, motorcycles, transmission fluids, greases, coolants, engine care and selected hydraulic/industrial products.

Customer channels

Resellers, garages, mechanics, fleets, automotive customers, mining, construction, industrial and commercial users.

Leadership

Abdoul Linkone — Founder & CEO
