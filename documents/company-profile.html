SBHL / COMPANY PROFILE

Société Bilal Hamiadou Linkoné

SBHL is an oil and lubricants trading and distribution business focused first on Burkina Faso, with a planned regional expansion toward Mali and wider West Africa.

Operating focus

SBHL serves lubricant resellers, garages, mechanics, transport and fleet operators, industrial users, mining, construction and commercial buyers.

Distribution model

Supply → Load → Ship → Distribute → Repeat. The model is designed around disciplined sourcing, shipment preparation, customer delivery and repeat-cycle planning.
