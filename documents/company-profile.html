SBHL / COMPANY PROFILE

Société Bilal Hamiadou Linkoné

SBHLOIL is an oil and lubricants trading and distribution business focused first on Burkina Faso, with a planned regional development direction toward Mali and wider West Africa.

Company overview

SBHL is developing a structured supply and distribution business around lubricant products for commercial customers. The operating focus is on dependable sourcing, organized logistics, customer delivery and repeat supply relationships.

Operating focus

SBHLOIL serves lubricant resellers, garages, mechanics, transport and fleet operators, industrial users, mining, construction and commercial buyers. Requirements are discussed according to application, equipment, volume, location and timing.

Market approach

The initial market focus is Burkina Faso. The longer-term business concept allows for regional development toward Mali and other West African markets as supply capacity, partnerships and customer demand develop.

Distribution model

Supply → Load → Ship → Distribute → Repeat. This model is designed to connect product sourcing with shipment preparation, transportation, customer delivery and the next supply cycle.

Customer relationship focus

SBHLOIL's commercial approach emphasizes clear product requirements, practical supply planning and repeatable customer support rather than publishing unverified product specifications or availability.
