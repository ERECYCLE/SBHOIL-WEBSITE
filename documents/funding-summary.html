SBHL / FUNDING SUMMARY

Launch capital and working capital.

SBHL's funding model is structured around initial product sourcing, logistics, commercial distribution activity and working capital for the first operating cycle.

Launch capital

SBHL has modeled a minimum viable launch scenario around USD 5,000 and an expanded working-capital scenario around USD 7,000. These figures are planning assumptions and can be adjusted as supplier terms, logistics costs and confirmed customer demand become clearer.

Use of capital

Funding is intended to support initial product sourcing, logistics and shipment-related costs, commercial distribution activity and working capital for the first operating cycle.

Product sourcingInitial lubricant purchasing and supplier-related costs.
LogisticsShipment preparation, transportation and related operating costs.
Working capitalLiquidity supporting the first sales and replenishment cycle.

Base operating concept

The planning model uses an initial container-per-month operating assumption, subject to supplier availability, logistics conditions and confirmed customer demand. Actual operating volumes should be updated as commercial commitments are established.

Funding discussion

A prospective funding partner can review the business plan, launch budget, supply assumptions, customer pipeline and operating model as part of a financing discussion.
