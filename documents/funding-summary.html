SBHL / FUNDING SUMMARY

Launch Capital

SBHL has modeled a minimum viable launch scenario around USD 5,000 and an expanded working-capital scenario around USD 7,000.

Use of capital

Funding is intended to support initial product sourcing, logistics and shipment-related costs, commercial distribution activity and working capital for the first operating cycle.

Base operating concept

The planning model uses an initial container-per-month operating assumption, subject to supplier availability, logistics conditions and confirmed customer demand.
