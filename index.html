ENGINE OIL

Products designed around real operating needs.

SBHOIL's commercial offering is centered on engine oil. Final product grades, specifications, packaging and availability are confirmed with suppliers and customers before each transaction.

Passenger & Light Commercial

Engine-oil supply for cars, SUVs, vans and light commercial vehicles. Grade and specification selected according to manufacturer requirements.

Heavy-Duty & Fleet

Mining & Industrial
